The model · four revenue engines

Arrivio is the settlement operating layer. Housing is the wedge: we lease and convert distressed hotels first, then offices, landlords fund the fit-out, and we never own the bricks. On top sit the asset-light engines: a partner network we place but never operate, employer-paid B2B settlement packages, and an embedded digital & financial layer. By Year 5 the asset-light engines are three-quarters of revenue and only one touches real estate.
